## Who We Are

The Azeroth Exchange believes that the Ministry of the Allied thrives when its citizens are free to thrive. We do not believe in carrying the capable. We do not believe in taxing the productive to subsidise the idle. We believe in reward for effort, opportunity for ambition, and a guild that gets out of the way of its strongest players and lets them lead.

We take our name from the principle that drives every great market: free exchange between free agents, each pursuing their own interest, creating something greater in the aggregate.

The best guilds are not managed. They are unleashed.

---

## What We Stand For

**Low taxation, high output.**
The Azeroth Exchange will keep guild taxes to the minimum required to maintain essential operations. Citizens keep what they earn. A lighter treasury burden means more gold in citizen pockets, more investment in personal progression, and a stronger server economy driven by our members.

**A free guild market.**
Resources flow through trade, not allocation. Under an Azeroth Exchange government, citizens buy and sell within the guild at market rates. No central committee decides who gets what. The best crafters, farmers, and traders rise naturally.

**Merit-based raiding.**
Raid slots go to the players who have earned them. Performance, preparation, and contribution determine your place in the raid — not seniority, not connections, not who your friends are in the cabinet. The strongest team wins. That is how progression happens.

**Elite investment.**
Rather than spreading thin support across all citizens equally, an Azeroth Exchange government directs guild investment toward those who demonstrate the highest output and the greatest potential. Raise the ceiling, and everyone below it rises too.

**Lean government.**
We will pass fewer laws, not more. Every law is a constraint on citizen freedom. An Azeroth Exchange government legislates only where necessary and sunsets laws that have outlived their purpose. The Ministry should enable its citizens, not administrate them.

## Our Promise

We will not punish your success. We will not redistribute your effort. We will build a guild where the best players want to be — because in a guild full of the best, everyone is better.

The Exchange is open. Come and build your legacy.

# The Silverhand — Party Manifesto

*Founded November 2026 — Ministry of the Allied*

---

> *"The strength of the light is not in the one who wields it, but in all those it protects."*

---

## Who We Are

The Silverhand believes that the Ministry of the Allied is only as strong as its weakest citizen. We are a guild — a community — not a collection of individuals who happen to share a tag. What we build, we build together. What we earn, we share with purpose. What we protect, we protect for everyone.

We take our name from the Order of the Silver Hand: paladins who gave everything in service of something greater than themselves. That is the spirit we carry into government.

---

## What We Stand For

**A funded commons.**
The guild treasury exists to serve the guild. Under a Silverhand government, treasury funds are used to support all citizens — raid consumables, crafting materials for new members, repairs for those who cannot yet sustain themselves. No citizen should fall behind because they are new.

**Collective raiding.**
Raid slots, loot, and progression are organised around the health of the whole raid — not the ambitions of the few. Loot distribution under a Silverhand government is council-guided and needs-based. The gear goes where it makes the raid stronger.

**Progressive taxation.**
Those who earn more contribute more. Tax rates scale with output, and the treasury is audited and published publicly each month. Citizens know exactly where their contributions go.

**Support for new citizens.**
Every new member of the Ministry is an investment. A Silverhand government funds starter support — bags, gold, guidance — to get new citizens productive as quickly as possible. A strong recruit today is a raid leader tomorrow.

**Transparent governance.**
Every law we pass, every coin we spend, every decision we make is published to the public record. Government under the Silverhand is open government.

---

## Our Promise

We will not govern for the top of the guild. We will not govern for our friends. We will govern for every citizen who logs in, puts in their time, and deserves a guild that has their back.

The light protects all. So do we.

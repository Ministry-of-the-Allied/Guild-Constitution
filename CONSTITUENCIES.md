# The Stormwind Constituency Map

*Ministry of the Allied — Regional Districts*
*Version 1.0 — November 2026*

---

## Overview

The Ministry of the Allied is organised into five regional districts, each anchored to a named location within Stormwind. Every citizen is assigned to a district upon joining the guild. Districts are not merely administrative divisions — they are communities, each with their own character, interests, and voice in government.

Each district elects a **Regional Representative** who sits on the **Regional Council**. The Council advises the government on matters affecting its constituents and must be formally consulted before legislation that primarily affects a specific district is passed.

Guild meetings, council sessions, and civic ceremonies are held in-world at each district's designated location. The geography of Stormwind is the geography of the Ministry.

---

## The Five Districts

---

### 1. The Old Quarter
**Location:** Old Town, Stormwind
**Character:** Military, defence, PvP, honour

Old Town is home to the Stormwind City Guard, the SI:7 rogues, and the fighters who keep the city's walls standing. The Old Quarter represents the Ministry's warriors, PvP players, and those who see guild strength through the lens of combat readiness.

Citizens of the Old Quarter believe the guild's primary obligation is to field the strongest possible fighting force. They tend to favour investment in raid progression and PvP resources, and hold strong opinions on loot distribution and raid composition.

**Council meeting location:** SI:7 Headquarters, Old Town
**Natural political lean:** Split — values both collective defence and individual martial excellence

---

### 2. The Arcane Ward
**Location:** Mage Quarter, Stormwind
**Character:** Crafting, enchanting, intellect, strategy

The Mage Quarter rises above the rest of Stormwind in towers of study and practice. The Arcane Ward represents the Ministry's crafters, enchanters, scribes, and strategic thinkers — those who believe that preparation and knowledge win wars before they are fought.

Citizens of the Arcane Ward tend to care deeply about the guild's resource policy, the law registry, and the long-term planning of the government. They are often the most engaged in legislative debate.

**Council meeting location:** Stormwind Mage Tower, Mage Quarter
**Natural political lean:** Silverhand — values collective preparation and shared knowledge

---

### 3. The Iron District
**Location:** Dwarven District, Stormwind
**Character:** Mining, blacksmithing, industry, production

The Dwarven District rings with the sound of hammers and forge fire. The Iron District represents the Ministry's producers — miners, blacksmiths, engineers, and those who generate the raw materials that fuel the guild's economy and its raids.

Citizens of the Iron District are the backbone of the guild's treasury. They have strong opinions on tax rates and how their output is used. A government that takes too much without delivering value will hear about it from the Iron District.

**Council meeting location:** AH steps, Dwarven District
**Natural political lean:** Azeroth Exchange — values fair reward for production and low interference

---

### 4. The Cathedral Ward
**Location:** Cathedral Square, Stormwind
**Character:** Justice, diplomacy, ceremony, civic life

Cathedral Square is Stormwind's spiritual and civic heart. The Cathedral Ward is the Ministry's most politically engaged district — home to the High Judiciary's deliberations, formal ceremonies, election announcements, and diplomatic meetings with other guilds.

Citizens of the Cathedral Ward tend to be drawn to governance itself: the rules, the record, the debate. They are often candidates for office, members of the judiciary, or the most vocal voices in any no-confidence process.

**Council meeting location:** Cathedral of Light, Cathedral Square
**Natural political lean:** Silverhand — values institution, fairness, and civic order

---

### 5. The Harbour Quarter
**Location:** Stormwind Harbour
**Character:** Trade, commerce, external affairs, exploration

The Harbour is where Stormwind meets the world. Ships arrive, goods are traded, and adventurers depart for distant shores. The Harbour Quarter represents the Ministry's traders, auctioneers, and those most focused on the guild's external relationships — with the server economy, with other guilds, and with the wider world.

Citizens of the Harbour Quarter care about the guild's reputation on the server, inter-guild diplomacy, and the health of the auction house economy. They are often the most outward-facing members of the community.

**Council meeting location:** Harbour Master's Office, Stormwind Harbour
**Natural political lean:** Azeroth Exchange — values open trade and external opportunity

---

## The Throne

**Location:** Stormwind Keep, The Trade District

Stormwind Keep is the seat of the Crown. All formal matters of state — election results, Royal Assent, dissolution announcements, no-confidence rulings, and ceremonial transitions of power — take place here.

The Throne is not a district. It belongs to all citizens and to none.

---

## District Assignment

New citizens are assigned to a district by the Minister of Recruitment upon joining. Assignment takes into account the citizen's class, primary activity, and stated preferences. Citizens may formally apply to transfer districts after a minimum of one full term, subject to approval by both the relevant Regional Representatives.

District populations are monitored to ensure no single district becomes disproportionately large. The Minister of Recruitment manages balance across the five districts as part of their portfolio.

---

## The Regional Council

The five Regional Representatives meet as the Regional Council. The Council:

- Must be consulted before legislation primarily affecting a specific district is passed
- May submit formal petitions to the government on behalf of their constituents
- May formally censure a cabinet minister through a majority council vote
- Publishes a public record of its sessions and positions

The Regional Council does not hold legislative power. It advises, scrutinises, and represents. Power remains with the elected government — but a government that ignores the Council does so at political cost.

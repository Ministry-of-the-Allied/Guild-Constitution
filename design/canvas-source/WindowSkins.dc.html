What the skin changes

Changes with every skin

  • The leather, with the Crown's soft purple glow behind the title
  • The resting and hover tabs, pressed in the skin's wax
  • The settings knob, and the close knob in the skin's wax or coin
  • The corner emblem: Crown crest, blue hand, coin-scale or lion crest
  • The ink of the lit tab's label
  • The pale ink: page name, the name at the foot of the rail, the status strip
  • The three corner jewels: sapphire, jade (Crown) or amethyst (Exchange)
  • The accent, for the Crown and the Exchange: headings, blue plates, blue buttons, lit ribbons and pills, the divider's jewel and the initials bubble turn plum or crimson. Silverhand keeps the Ministry blue

Changes with the metal

  • The outer rim, the double rule and the stitching
  • The three filigree corners
  • The title, "Ministry of the Allied"
  • The lit tab and its tooled border
  • The metal rim round the parchment page

Gold for the Crown, the Exchange and the Ministry; silver for Silverhand. So there are two sets, not four.

Stays the same

  • The parchment itself, the seals, the gold buttons, the party chips and the district colours
  • The tab icons (drawn once in white, coloured in the game); the tab names are live game text, with a fine dark shadow on the resting tabs
  • Where things sit: in the add-on the title, page name and knobs sit 6 lower than the stylesheet, and the status strip's words 5 lower and 36 further in
  • The window's shape, size and shadow
  • The heralds, the settings page and the minimap seal

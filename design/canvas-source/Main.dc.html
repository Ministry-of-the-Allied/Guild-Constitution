Ministry of the Allied · Add-on visual language

The Royal Seal

The add-on should feel like the official instruments of the Stormwind court: state papers on heavy parchment, bound in royal blue leather and gold, closed with wax.

Gold is always metal
Every official act gets a seal
One window, six pages

I · The three materials

Royal Leather

The window frame, the tab rail and headers. Deep blue hide with tooled gold lines and a stitched inner border.

Burnished Gold

Borders, trims, the crest and anything active. Always a metallic gradient: highlight, warm body, brown shadow. Never flat yellow.

Parchment

Every content page. Warm vellum with grain, fibres and age-darkened edges, so text reads like ink on a state paper.
